A quiet corner of a surf town

Hiriketiya is a small horseshoe bay on Sri Lanka’s south coast, known for easy waves and a relaxed crowd. Surf Home Stay sits right on it, a calmer spot than the busy main beach, with a view of the surfers from your breakfast table.

Stay in one of 8 bedrooms, have the big Sri Lankan breakfast, then spend the day on the sand. In the evening the café lights come on and dinner is served beside the water.
